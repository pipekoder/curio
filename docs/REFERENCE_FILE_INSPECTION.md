## Fidelity constraints established before coding

Preserve the two selected screens' signature positions, scale relationships,
planes, floor and input/caption geometry. Use approved Curio originals where the
photographs lack independently established publication rights. That substitution
is a major imagery/depth discrepancy and prevents claiming photographic fidelity.
Do not manufacture a cutout/mask or material close-up from an illustration.

The prototype is opt-in and reuses the existing editorial state/requests, validation,
permissions, story and Collect handlers. Generate/Journey/Collect retain their
existing functional presentation; their redesign awaits explicit visual approval.
Exact reference-sized browser screenshots are compared with private original files
locally. No composite containing reference photographs is committed publicly.
